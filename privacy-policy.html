Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters to us. This policy explains how Vistara Solutions collects, uses and protects information shared through our website.

Introduction

Vistara Solutions respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the information you share with us. This Privacy Policy explains how we may collect, use and safeguard information when you visit our website or contact us regarding our real estate services.

When you interact with our website, you may voluntarily provide information such as your name, phone number, email address and property requirements.
